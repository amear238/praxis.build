If the trader moved more than one Pro account to Live, the Reserves pay out as one bonus. The payout threshold goes up with the number of accounts moved.
2. Live Account Hits the Max Loss Limit
The Reserve pays off the negative Live balance.
Any Reserve left over goes back to the Dormant Sim Funded account. The trader can only withdraw from it. The trader cannot trade it.
Profit above the buffer is paid at 80/20.
The buffer is paid based on how long the Sim Funded account was traded:
Fewer than 30 trading days: 50% of the buffer.
30 trading days or more: 80% of the buffer.
After that withdrawal, the Sim Funded account closes.
A 21-day cooldown starts.
Example (Pro 50K): The Reserve holds $5,000. The Live account closes at -$1,500.
$1,500 comes out of the Reserve to cover the Live loss.
$3,500 goes back to the Dormant Sim Funded account.
The first $2,100 of that is the buffer. The account was traded for fewer than 30 days, so the trader receives 50%: $1,050.
The remaining $1,400 is above the buffer. The trader receives 80%: $1,120.
Live Post-Breach Cooldown
For 21 calendar days after the Live account closes:
The trader cannot trade any Sim Funded account.
The trader cannot buy new evaluations, resets or accounts.
After 21 days, the restrictions end. Dormant accounts are turned back on.

Account Rules
How the EOD Trailing Drawdown Works
The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is an End of Day (EOD) trailing drawdown. It does not move intraday. It only adjusts after the market closes each session, based on where your account closed.
The MLL trails upward as your account equity reaches new end-of-day highs.
It never moves down.
The MLL locks permanently once it reaches $100 above the starting balance. From that point, it does not trail further.
How the Soft Daily Loss Limit Works
The Soft DLL ($1,750 on the Builder 100K) is a soft pause, not a breach. If your intraday losses reach the Soft DLL, trading stops for the remainder of that trading session. Your account remains active, and you resume the next trading session as normal.
It applies during both the Evaluation and Sim Funded stages. The only rule that can close your account is breaching the MLL.
News Trading
T1 news trading is allowed during the Evaluation and Sim Funded stages. You may open and hold positions through any scheduled T1 news event without limitation.

FAQ
What is the difference between Studio #001, #002 and #003?
Same plan shape at two sizes:
#001 is $10K at $140 (MLL $500, 10 micros, $5K daily profit cap).
#002 is $20K at $280 (MLL $1,000, 20 micros, $10K daily profit cap).
#003 is $5k at $75 (MLL $250, 5 micros, $2,500 daily profit cap).
Rules and payouts are otherwise the same.
How does the Max Loss Limit work?
It is static. It is set when the account starts and never moves during Evaluation or Sim Funded.
When can I request a payout?
Anytime on Sim Funded once your positions are closed.
Minimum $250 on #001 and #002
Minimum $100 on #003
Is there a limit on daily profits?
Yes, on Sim Funded only. Net realized profit above $5,000 (#001), $10,000 (#002) or $2,500 (#003) in a single trading day is forfeited.
Can I have more than one Studio account?
No. One active Studio account at a time — either Evaluation or Sim Funded.
Which platform do Studio accounts use?
Hyperprop only. More platforms coming soon.
When do I become eligible for Live?
After 5 approved payouts or $5,000 total requested. Live starts at 40% of total requested, up to $2,000. Our team may also move you earlier.
​Are there activation fees?
No. There are no activation fees for any of our Studio Plans.
Is news trading allowed?
Yes.
