PRODUCT_DESCRIPTION: Daily payouts with a 90/10 split and no consistency rule in the funded stage. Pass in 2 days, $0 activation fee. Intraday trailing drawdown — explained upfront.
Q: How does the 90/10 profit split work on the Rapid plan?
A: You keep 90% of every payout. MFFU keeps 10%. There's no graduated tier, no 'first $X at one rate, then a different rate.' Every payout you request on a Rapid account follows the same 90/10 split.
Q: How fast can I get my first payout on Rapid?
A: The evaluation requires two trading days. Once funded, you need to build the required buffer ($2,100 on the $50K account) in realized profits before your first payout request. After that threshold is cleared, you can request every 24 hours. For most traders, first payout eligibility comes a few days into the funded stage — it depends entirely on how quickly you build the buffer.
Q: What is intraday trailing drawdown?
A: Intraday trailing drawdown means your max-loss floor moves up with your equity in real time during the session — not just at end-of-day. On Rapid Sim Funded, once your balance is +$100 above your starting balance after your first payout, the floor locks and stops trailing upward permanently.
Q: Does the Rapid plan have a daily loss limit?
A: No. Rapid has no daily loss limit in either the evaluation or the funded stage. The only loss constraint is your max drawdown — that's it.
Q: What's the difference between Rapid and the other MFFU plans?
A: Rapid is built for speed: daily payouts, 2-day pass, 90/10 split, $0 activation fee. Builder is the low-cost entry plan with a structured 5-payout ladder to a live brokerage seat. Pro has the largest account sizes and highest per-payout cap. Flex is a separate plan available in $25K and $50K sizes — it has different payout mechanics. Rapid is the plan MFFU recommends for traders who want the fastest payout cadence and best profit split.
Q: Is there an activation fee on Rapid?
A: No. Rapid has zero activation fees at every account size. When you pass the evaluation, your funded account activates instantly — you don't pay anything additional to unlock it.
Q: Can I hold positions overnight on Rapid?
A: No. Rapid is intended for intraday futures trading. All positions must be closed before the daily session close. Holding overnight will breach the account.
Q: What happens if I hit the max loss on Rapid?
A: On a Rapid Evaluation, breaching the max drawdown fails that evaluation, but you can reset it for a specific fee and start trading again. On a Rapid Sim Funded account, breaching the max drawdown closes the account. Funded accounts can't be reset, so you'll need to start a new evaluation. The max drawdown is a hard line, so we recommend trading well above it.
Q: Why is Tier 1 news trading restricted on Rapid?
A: Tier 1 news events (CPI, NFP, FOMC, etc.) create extreme short-term volatility that can blow out simulated accounts before stop-losses fill. To keep the simulated market behavior representative of live execution risk, T1 events are restricted within 2 minutes before and after the release.
Q: How many Rapid accounts can I have at the same time?
A: For $25K and $50K Rapid accounts, you can hold up to five active sim funded accounts at once. For $100K and $150K accounts, the limit is three total sim funded accounts across all plans.

FAQ
Questions about getting started.
What's the difference between Builder and the other MFFU plans?
Builder is the easiest path to funding in the prop space and the only MFFU plan with a fixed payout ladder to live capital — five rungs, each up to $2,000, then a real brokerage account. It has a one-day minimum evaluation, a $1,000 soft-pause daily loss limit, and an 80/20 split. Rapid pays daily on sim with a 90/10 split and no fixed ladder. Pro has the highest payout cap at $100K and its own path to live.
How does the Builder payout ladder actually work?
After you pass the evaluation, you trade a sim-funded Builder account through up to five approved payout cycles. Before your first payout, you need to clear the required buffer — $2,100 on the default plan, $1,600 on the add-on. Once the buffer is cleared, each payout cycle requires a minimum of 2 qualifying trading days, at least $500 net profit since your last payout, and must fall between $500 and $2,000. A 50% consistency rule applies at the payout stage — your single best day can't account for more than half of that cycle's profits. After your fifth approved sim payout, you graduate to a live brokerage account at Blue Row Capital with daily payouts, an 80/20 split, and no consistency rule.
What is a "soft pause" daily loss limit?
When your session hits $1,000 down for the day, trading pauses until the next session — but the account is not breached. It is a coaching mechanism for traders still building discipline, not a hard kill switch. You return the next day with the account intact.
When do I graduate from sim funded to live on Builder?
After five approved sim payouts on your Builder account. The fifth approved payout triggers promotion to a live account underwritten by Blue Row Capital.
Can I have more than one Builder account at the same time?
No. Builder is limited to one active Builder account per user. The plan is built for focus, not for stacking multiple evals.
What happens if I breach on Builder?
If you breach max drawdown on the sim-funded Builder account, the account ends and you start a new evaluation. Hitting the $1,000 daily soft-pause limit only ends your session for that day — it does not breach the account. Note: if you breach after graduating to a live account at Blue Row Capital, a 21-day cooldown applies — no sim funded trading, no new eval purchases, and no resets during that period.
Is Builder good for beginners?
Yes — Builder is designed as the easiest entry path to MFFU with forgiving EOD trailing drawdown, a one-day pass minimum, and a clear five-payout path to live capital. The soft-pause daily loss limit gives traders room to learn without a single bad session ending the account.
How is Builder different from other low-cost one-step plans?
Most low-cost one-step plans stop at sim. Builder includes a documented five-payout ladder to a real live brokerage seat, $0 activation fee, EOD trailing drawdown (not intraday), and a soft-pause DLL that keeps the account alive after a bad day.
What does the live Builder account look like?
After five approved sim payouts, you trade live capital at Blue Row Capital. The live account carries an EOD trailing drawdown of $2,000 (default plan) or $1,500 (add-on plan), which becomes static once the MLL reaches $0. You get daily payouts, an 80/20 split, a $1,000 daily loss limit, no consistency rule, and one live account per user. A 21-day cooldown applies if the live account is breached.
