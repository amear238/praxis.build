# Opening range breakout on the $150,000 frame: design

Date: 2026-11-17. Asked for by Amear in the Claude Code session: "write a plan for the opening range breakout, with the account test as the problem to solve ... go. test like you did for the superpowers plan for noise area breakout." So the plan is written and run in this session.

## Why this one

Of everything tested besides Noise-Area, the 5-minute opening range breakout (candidate 2, branch `worktree-candidate-2-orb`) is the only one whose average winner cleared twice the risk (about 3.2 times) while staying profitable ($8,055 on the $50,000 stand-in, 1,348 trades, 24% winners, profit factor 1.02, harsh fills). It failed only on survival: at 1% risk its losing runs crossed the $2,000 limit in the first week and in every shuffle. It has a real stop (the far side of the range) and trades nearly every day.

## Goal

Find out whether any variation of the opening range breakout passes all five bars on the $150,000 MyFundedFutures evaluation frame. Name a finalist, or say plainly that none passed.

## The problem to solve: survival

Both the profit bar ($9,000) and the survival bar ($4,500 trailing) are in dollars and both scale with the risk per trade. Lowering the risk alone cannot pass both. What decides a pass is a number that does not depend on the size: the profit, divided by the drawdown the account takes in the worst shuffles. So:

1. The measuring pass reports, for each rule, the profit in units of risk (R) and the drawdown in R that only 2.5% of shuffled orders exceed.
2. Each variation's risk percentage is **set from the 2021 to 2023 measuring pass**, as the size at which 2.5% of shuffles cross $4,500 (half the 5% bar, as margin for the later years and for the risk growing with the balance). It is not tuned on the full period.
3. The variations are chosen for the rules that make the most R per R of drawdown: fewer, better trades, smaller losing runs.

## Not goals

- No NinjaTrader work, no order code, no live or sim trading.
- No tuning of a finalist. That is the next stage.
- No other strategy family.

## What would make this a failure even if it "works"

- A variation picked as the best of many (luck). The report states how many rules were measured and how many variations were run.
- Look-ahead: the opening range, the daily range average and every decision use only bars already closed.
- A loss at the stop above the planned cost, other than a gap through the stop.
- Bars moved after seeing results.
- 2024 to 2026 is not truly unseen: candidate 2 ran over it. The report says so.

## The frame and the bars (unchanged from the Noise-Area $150,000 rerun)

The $150,000 MyFundedFutures Builder evaluation, as read from their site on 2026-11-17: a **$4,500** limit that trails the end-of-day balance, a **$9,000** target (`research/noise-area-variations/variations-150k/LIST.md`). Judged on **midpoint fills**. Kind and harsh fills reported beside.

| Bar | Rule |
|---|---|
| Rule check | Every simulated order matches the variation's written rule; every entry has a stop-market order; a second run gives the same trades. |
| 2-to-1 | Average R of winning trades at least 2.0, over all years and over 2024-01-01 on; and 2024 on makes a profit. |
| Frequency | No more than seven trading days in a row without an entry. |
| Profit | Net profit at least $9,000. |
| Survival | The $4,500 end-of-day trailing limit never crossed in the real order of days, and crossed in at most 5% of 10,000 shuffled orders (seed 20261115). |

Information only, as before: the lock rule (the limit stops $100 above the start), evaluations started on each day, most micros held (90 allowed), days down $2,500 or more.

## The rule and its levers

Data: the held NQ 1-minute file, 2021-03-08 to 2026-07-17, Eastern stamps marking the end of the minute. Instrument MNQ: $2 a point, $1.90 a contract round trip, 2 ticks slippage per side on market fills. Regular hours only: no entry decided before the opening range is complete, flat at the 15:59 bar (filled at the 16:00 open).

Each variation is one settings file:

| Field | Values | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `or_minutes` | 5, 15, 30 | The opening range: bars stamped 09:31 to 09:30 + n. |
| `entry` | `direction`, `break` | `direction` (the published rule): the range's close vs its open sets the side, market order at the next bar's open; a range that closes where it opened gives no trade. `break`: the first bar after the range, up to `entry_until`, that closes above the range high (long) or below its low (short); market order at the next bar's open. |
| `entry_until` | stamp, for `break` | Last bar on which a break may be decided. |
| `sides` | `both`, `long`, `short` | |
| `stop` | `far_side`, `or_frac` x, `atr_frac` x | Stop distance from the decision close: to the far side of the range; x times the range's height; x times the 14-day average of the regular-hours daily range (the 14 previous days). |
| `exit` | `hold_to_flat`, `target_r` x, `trail_r` x | Held to 15:59; a resting limit at x times the stop distance; or a stop trailed x times the stop distance behind the best price since entry. |
| `max_or_atr` | null or x | No trade when the range's height is above x times the 14-day average daily range. |
| `risk_pct` | percent | The set cost of the trade, a percentage of the current balance. |

One trade a day at most. Size as in the Noise-Area engine: loss per contract = (stop distance + 0.5) x 2 + 1.90; contracts = floor(balance x risk_pct / 100 / that), at most 90; under one contract or a stop under 10 points: skipped and counted. The stop rests that distance from the actual entry fill. A target fills at its price when a bar trades one tick through it. Stop and target touched in the same minute: the stop.

## Stages

1. **Build and check** the engine and an independent rule check.
2. **Measure** on 2021-03-08 to 2023-12-31 only: every combination of `or_minutes` (3) x `entry` (2) x `stop` (far side, 0.5 of the range, 0.10 of the daily range) x `exit` (hold, 3R, 5R, 10R target, trail 1R, trail 2R), both sides, one micro a trade. Per rule: trades, win rate, average winner in R, net R, net R by year and by side, longest losing run, longest gap in trading days, the 97.5th-percentile shuffled drawdown in R, the safe dollars per R (4,500 / that), and the projected full-period profit at that size. Also net R by range-height tercile, for the `max_or_atr` lever.
3. **Fix the list**: at most eight variations, each with its risk percentage from stage 2 and a one-line reason, committed before any run. If none passes, one second list of at most eight, from 2021 to 2023 evidence only. Then the question goes to Amear.
4. **Run and judge** on the full period, three fills, two runs each.
5. **Verdict** in plain English; status updated.
